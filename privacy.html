PeakCut Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

PeakCut provides video processing and social media distribution tools. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when you use PeakCut.

2. Information We May Collect

  • Name and email address.
  • Videos and media files you upload.
  • Social media account identifiers and authorization tokens when you connect accounts.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

3. Instagram and Meta

If you connect an Instagram account, PeakCut may receive account information and permissions authorized through Meta. PeakCut does not ask you to provide your Instagram password directly.

4. How Information Is Used

  • To provide PeakCut features.
  • To process videos and media.
  • To connect social media accounts.
  • To publish content when you request it.
  • To maintain security and troubleshoot problems.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with platforms and service providers when necessary to provide requested features, operate PeakCut, or comply with legal requirements.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information. Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.
